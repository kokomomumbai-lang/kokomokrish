Matcha in Santacruz East.

Cafe Kokomo's matcha bar has five drinks, all ₹299, made with select-grade matcha.

The five drinks

  • Iced Matcha: matcha with chilled milk. No added sugar.
  • Iced Coconut Matcha: matcha with coconut and chilled milk. No added sugar.
  • Iced Strawberry Glow Matcha: matcha with strawberry and chilled milk.
  • Iced Velvet Vanilla Matcha: matcha with vanilla and chilled milk.
  • Hot Velvet Vanilla Matcha: a warm matcha latte with vanilla and steamed milk.

Which one to order

If you want to taste the matcha itself, start with the Iced Matcha or the Iced Coconut Matcha, which have no added sugar. Strawberry Glow and Velvet Vanilla add strawberry or vanilla, and the Velvet Vanilla is the one to order hot.

Something to eat with it

A Butter Croissant (₹249), a Honey Glazed Cinnamon Roll (₹219) or a slice of New York Cheesecake (₹299). Everything is on the full menu.

Ordering in

Iced Matcha is also on our Swiggy and Zomato menus. Delivery prices are set separately from the dine-in prices here.
