The space

Come for the coffee. Stay for that wall.

A cozy, tropical-themed cafe, and the black-and-white line-art mural is the first thing you see when you walk in. It's also the thing most people photograph.

Round black tables, white chairs, plants along the glass front, and a counter by the window for a quick coffee on your own.

Behind the counter: single-origin beans and custom roasts, latte art with award-winning microfoam, and a few greens we grow ourselves.

What time is Cafe Kokomo open?

Every day, 11 AM to 11 PM.

Where exactly is the cafe?

4, Pratap CHS Ltd, Shivaji Road, near Vakola Bridge, Santacruz East, Mumbai 400055. It's an 11-minute walk from Nap Manor Hostels.

Do I need to book a table?

You can walk in. To book ahead, use Book a table on this site: it opens WhatsApp with your request and we reply to confirm.

Can I book for a group or a celebration?

Yes. For 7 or more people, choose 7+ guests when you book and tell us the occasion in the notes, so we can set up the tables.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. 106 of the 130 items on the menu are vegetarian. Every dish carries a dot: green for veg, yellow for egg and red for non-veg, and the menu page has a Veg only filter.

Is there Wi-Fi? Can I work from the cafe?

Yes. There's Wi-Fi and plug points, and you're welcome to stay through the afternoon.

Do you serve breakfast all day?

Yes. The all-day breakfast includes the English Breakfast, omelettes, scrambled eggs and bagels.

Do you have matcha?

Yes, five matcha drinks at ₹299: Iced Matcha, Iced Coconut Matcha, Iced Strawberry Glow Matcha, and the Velvet Vanilla Matcha, hot or iced.

Do you have anything gluten-free?

The World's Best Chocolate Cake is marked gluten-free on our menu (it contains egg). Ask us about other dishes.

Do you deliver?

Yes, through Swiggy and Zomato. Delivery prices are set separately from the dine-in prices on this site.

How can I pay?

Cash, card or UPI.

What are Sip & Paint and Blind Date Book?

Sip & Paint (₹149) gives you a canvas, brushes and paints at your table, and the painting goes home with you. Blind Date Book (₹199) is a hand-wrapped surprise book in the genre you choose.

How much does a meal cost?

An espresso is ₹149 and most pastas and sandwiches are ₹329 to ₹399. A meal for two usually comes to about ₹400 to ₹600.
